# NEC Key Articles Quick Reference (NFPA 70)

## General Requirements
- **Art. 90** – Introduction: Purpose, scope, enforcement
- **Art. 100** – Definitions
- **Art. 110** – General requirements: working clearances (110.26), arc flash labels (110.16), equipment ratings

## Wiring Methods & Materials
- **Art. 200** – Use and identification of grounded conductors
- **Art. 210** – Branch circuits: AFCI (210.12), GFCI (210.8), load calc (210.19)
- **Art. 215** – Feeders
- **Art. 220** – Branch-circuit, feeder, and service load calculations
- **Art. 230** – Services: clearances, disconnecting means, service entrance
- **Art. 240** – Overcurrent protection: fuses, circuit breakers, coordination
- **Art. 250** – Grounding and bonding (critical for all systems)

## Conductors & Cables
- **Art. 310** – Conductors for general wiring (Table 310.16 = ampacity table)
- **Art. 330–398** – Specific wiring methods (conduit, cable tray, etc.)

## Equipment for General Use
- **Art. 408** – Switchboards, switchgear, panelboards, MCC
- **Art. 409** – Industrial control panels
- **Art. 430** – Motors: full-load current tables, overload protection, disconnecting means
- **Art. 440** – HVAC equipment
- **Art. 445** – Generators: nameplate, disconnecting means, grounding
- **Art. 450** – Transformers: overcurrent protection, installation clearances
- **Art. 460** – Capacitors: power factor correction
- **Art. 480** – Storage batteries: venting, charging, disconnecting means

## Special Occupancies
- **Art. 500–516** – Hazardous (classified) locations
- **Art. 517** – Healthcare facilities: essential electrical system
- **Art. 518** – Places of assembly
- **Art. 547** – Agricultural buildings
- **Art. 553** – Floating buildings

## Special Equipment
- **Art. 620** – Elevators and escalators
- **Art. 625** – Electric vehicle supply equipment (EVSE)
- **Art. 645** – Information technology equipment (data centers)
- **Art. 680** – Swimming pools, fountains
- **Art. 690** – Solar PV systems (key for renewable projects)
  - 690.7: Maximum voltage
  - 690.8: Circuit sizing and current
  - 690.11: Arc fault protection
  - 690.12: Rapid shutdown
  - 690.47: Grounding
- **Art. 691** – Large-scale PV electric supply stations
- **Art. 694** – Wind electric systems
- **Art. 706** – Energy storage systems (ESS/BESS)
- **Art. 710** – Stand-alone systems

## Special Conditions (Emergency/Standby Power)
- **Art. 700** – Emergency systems (legally mandated, life safety)
- **Art. 701** – Legally required standby systems
- **Art. 702** – Optional standby systems
- **Art. 708** – Critical operations power systems (COPS) — data centers, 911, EOCs
